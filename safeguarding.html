Child safety and privacy

Children deserve respect and privacy in person and online. These principles can guide visitors, volunteers, and supporters.

Photos and stories

Ask the home before taking or sharing photos. Keep children’s names, personal stories, school details, and routines private.

Visits and volunteering

Arrange visits in advance and follow the home’s guidance on supervision and contact with children.

Explore volunteer roles

Concerns about a child

Share concerns privately through a verified child protection or safeguarding channel. Keep names and details out of public comments and social media. If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
